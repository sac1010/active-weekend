Board games have undergone a massive global renaissance, and Bangalore is right at the center of the trend. Across Koramangala, Indiranagar, and HSR Layout, board game meetups are replacing traditional movies or shopping malls as the go-to weekend social hangout.

If you are looking to play everything from Catan and Ticket to Ride to complex strategy games, here are the top 4 board game cafes in Bangalore.

## 1. The Board Game Lounge (HSR Layout)
This dedicated space in HSR Layout features a massive library of over 300+ board games. The staff are seasoned game masters who will happily recommend games based on your group size and teach you the rules.
- **Vibe**: Cozy, social, and beginner-friendly.
- **Key Tip**: They host regular social meetups on weekends where solo players can easily merge into open tables.

## 2. Dice N Dine (Koramangala)
Located in the heart of Koramangala, Dice N Dine is one of Bangalore's oldest and most popular board game cafes. They offer a great Italian menu alongside a large collection of games, ranging from party games to heavy strategy titles.
- **Vibe**: Energetic, food-focused, and casual.
- **Key Tip**: Table space can fill up fast on Saturday evenings. We recommend heading there early in the afternoon.

## 3. Play Zone Cafe (Indiranagar)
Tucked away in a quiet lane in Indiranagar, Play Zone combines specialty coffee with a curated shelf of modern board games. It is a fantastic spot for a quiet weekend afternoon session of Carcassonne or Splendor.
- **Vibe**: Relaxed, minimalist, and coffee-focused.
- **Key Tip**: Try their cold brews while playing—they are excellent!

## 4. Boards & Bites (Bellandur)
A popular hangout for tech professionals in the Bellandur IT corridor, Boards & Bites features large tables specifically designed for complex map-based board games.
- **Vibe**: Friendly, spacious, and community-driven.
- **Key Tip**: If you are a solo player, check their notice board or coordinate with local players online beforehand.

## How to Find Board Game Tables
Showing up to a board game lounge alone can be intimidating. If you want to join an active table or host a game night yourself, use the **ActiveWeekend app**. 

Select the "Board Games" category, find a host table looking for players in Koramangala or HSR, and secure your seat. It is the easiest way to jump straight into the fun with a friendly group!

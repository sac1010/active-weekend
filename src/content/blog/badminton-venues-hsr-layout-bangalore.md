Badminton is easily one of Bangalore's most popular indoor sports. With work hours keeping people busy during the week, securing a court booking in HSR Layout on a Saturday morning has become a sport in itself!

If you are looking to book a slot or join a game, here are the top 5 badminton venues in HSR Layout based on court quality, lighting, and ventilation.

## 1. HSR Club Badminton Courts
Located in the heart of Sector 3, HSR Club features two premium indoor wooden courts. They are well-maintained with excellent lighting, making it a favorite for seasoned players. Because it is a private club, booking slots directly can be tough unless you are a member or join a resident's roster group.

## 2. Play Arena (Sarjapur Road / HSR Border)
Technically right on the border of HSR and Sarjapur Road, Play Arena is a massive multi-sport venue with 4 indoor synthetic courts. The atmosphere here is always energetic, and they have premium facilities including showers and changing rooms. We recommend booking slots at least 3 days in advance on weekends.

## 3. Dribble Arena
Tucked away in Sector 2, Dribble Arena is a great budget-friendly option. It offers synthetic courts that are softer on the knees. Lighting is adequate, and the ceiling height is decent, though it can get slightly warm during afternoon slots.

## 4. The Smash Arena
Located near Sector 7, The Smash Arena features 3 wooden courts with synthetic overlays. It is very popular for intermediate matches. They also have an on-site sports shop where you can purchase grips, strings, or buy standard Yonex Mavis 350 plastic shuttlecocks.

## 5. Play Zone HSR
Play Zone offers basic synthetic courts. It is ideal for casual doubles games with friends. While the court mats are slightly older, it is easier to secure last-minute slots here compared to other premium arenas in HSR.

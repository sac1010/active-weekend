If you are a tennis, badminton, or table tennis player looking to pick up a paddle, here is everything you need to know to get started with Pickleball in Bangalore.

## What is Pickleball?
Pickleball is a hybrid of tennis, badminton, and table tennis. It is played on a badminton-sized court with a slightly modified tennis net, using solid paddles and a perforated plastic ball (wiffle ball). It is easy for beginners to learn, but highly strategic at advanced levels.

## Key Rules to Know
- **The Kitchen**: The 7-foot non-volley zone near the net is called "the kitchen". You cannot step inside the kitchen to volley the ball (hit it in the air)—you must let it bounce first.
- **Double Bounce Rule**: When the ball is served, the receiving team must let it bounce before hitting it. Similarly, the serving team must let the return bounce once before playing it. After that, volleys are allowed.
- **Scoring**: You can only score points when your team is serving. Games are typically played to 11 points (must win by 2).

For Bangalore's motorcycling community, weekends are meant for one thing: early morning breakfast rides. Escaping the city traffic at 6:00 AM on a Saturday, catching the cool breeze, and cruising along empty national highways is the ultimate weekend reset.

If you are looking to twist the throttle and explore, here are the 5 best weekend motorcycle routes from Bangalore under 150km.

## 1. Nandi Hills Sunrise Cruise (60km)
It is a classic for a reason. The ride up Nandi Hills features 40 hairpin curves, offering riders a quick, technical climb. The highway from Hebbal to the base of the hills is smooth, making it ideal for groups.
- **Key Tip**: The entry gate opens at 6:00 AM. Arrive early to beat the massive crowd of tourist cars.

## 2. Anchetty Loop via Kanakapura (130km)
If you want to escape the crowd, the Anchetty loop is Bangalore's best-kept riding secret. The route takes you through rural village roads, winding valleys, and thick forest sections.
- **Key Tip**: The roads are narrow and have sharp curves. Maintain moderate speeds and enjoy the scenic greenery.

## 3. DD Hills (Devarayanadurga) (75km)
Located near Tumkur, Devarayanadurga is a rocky hill station surrounded by forests. The ride features smooth highway cruising on NH48 followed by a short, winding hill climb to the hilltop temple.
- **Key Tip**: Stop at the local eateries near the base for hot "tatidte idli" (a Tumkur specialty) for breakfast.

## 4. Shoolagiri (NH44 South) (60km)
For pure, uninterrupted highway cruising, head south on NH44 towards Shoolagiri. The road is a wide, 6-lane highway flanked by scenic rock formations.
- **Key Tip**: This route is famous for its massive breakfast hubs, including popular spots like Krishna Grand and McD, making it a perfect quick morning run.

## 5. Manchanabele Dam (40km)
Located off the Mysore Road highway, Manchanabele offers a scenic ride along the backwaters of the Arkavati river. The route features rolling hills and curves.
- **Key Tip**: The roads near the dam can have loose gravel. Ride carefully and enjoy the view of the massive Savandurga monolith in the background.

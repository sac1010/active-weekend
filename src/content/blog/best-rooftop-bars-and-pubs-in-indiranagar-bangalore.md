# Top 7 Rooftop Bars & Craft Breweries in Indiranagar & Koramangala for Weekend Hangouts

Bangalore's legendary nightlife and craft beer culture make it India's undisputed pub capital. From sprawling open-air rooftop lounges in Indiranagar to vibrant brewpubs in Koramangala, the city offers unmatched social energy for weekend hangouts.

Whether you're organizing a weekend meetup for new friends or catching up over fresh IPAs with your sports squad, here are the top 7 rooftop bars and craft breweries in Bangalore to visit this weekend.

---

## 1. Toit Microbrewery (Indiranagar)

**Vibe:** Classic Pub Energy & Artisanal Craft Beer  
**Best For:** Craft beer enthusiasts, wood-fired pizzas, and vibrant group conversations.

Located in the heart of 100 Feet Road, Indiranagar, **Toit** is an iconic pioneer in Bangalore’s craft brewing scene. Known for signature brews like *Toit Tintin* (Belgian Witbier) and *Colonial IPA*, its multi-level rustic interior and energetic outdoor seating make it a favorite for weekend social meetups.

*   **Must Try:** Toit Tintin, Basmati Blonde, Wood-fired Pepperoni Pizza.
*   **Pro Tip:** Weekend queues are notoriously long—reach by 6:30 PM or organize your group meetup on ActiveWeekend to arrive early together.

---

## 2. Byg Brewski Brewing Company (Sarjapur & Hennur)

**Vibe:** Colossal Open-Air Amphitheater & Lakeside Lounging  
**Best For:** Large squad hangouts, live DJ sets, and romantic rooftop evenings.

As one of Asia's largest brewpubs, **Byg Brewski** offers a breathtaking open-air amphitheater set around a tranquil koi pond. The rooftop sections provide stunning views of Bangalore’s evening skyline paired with fresh craft ales and global street food.

*   **Must Try:** Byg Triple, Craft Wheat Ale, Malnad Mutton Chops.
*   **Location:** Sarjapur Road & Hennur Ring Road.

---

## 3. Arbor Brewing Company - ABC (Magrath Road, Central Bangalore)

**Vibe:** American Craft Brewery & Relaxed Community Pub  
**Best For:** Board game nights, trivia meetups, and classic American craft beers.

**Arbor Brewing Company (ABC)** brings authentic Michigan craft brewing to Central Bangalore. With large wooden communal tables and a laid-back atmosphere, ABC is ideal for group conversations, post-badminton drinks, and weekend social mixers.

*   **Must Try:** Smooth Criminal, Raging Elephant IPA, Loaded Nachos.

---

## 4. The Black Rabbit (Indiranagar)

**Vibe:** Chic Industrial Loft & Rooftop Cocktail Bar  
**Best For:** Gourmet burgers, artisanal cocktails, and high-energy weekend nights.

Situated right off 12th Main Indiranagar, **The Black Rabbit** features sleek industrial architecture with exposed brickwork, warm Edison lighting, and an airy rooftop space. It's ideal for casual Saturday night catchups over signature gin cocktails and gourmet sliders.

*   **Must Try:** Smoked Bacon Burger, Botanical Gin Tonics, Truffle Fries.

---

## 5. Windmills Craftworks (Whitefield)

**Vibe:** Jazz Lounge, Microbrewery & High-End Rooftop Sanctuary  
**Best For:** Premium craft beers, live jazz performances, and relaxed Sunday sessions.

For those in Whitefield or ITPL, **Windmills Craftworks** offers a sophisticated blend of world-class microbrewing, a curated library, and live music performance space. Their rooftop dining section provides a serene escape from urban traffic.

*   **Must Try:** Stout, Hefeweizen, Craft Sausages.

---

## 6. Social (Koramangala & Indiranagar)

**Vibe:** Creative Co-Working by Day, High-Octane Bar by Night  
**Best For:** Affordable cocktails, fusion street food, and spontaneous group hangouts.

**Koramangala Social** and **Indiranagar Social** are urban culture hubs featuring green indoor plants, neon decor, and spacious outdoor seating. Known for signature *LLITs (Longest Long Island Iced Teas)*, Social is perfect for young professionals looking to meet new people in Bangalore.

*   **Must Try:** Social LIIT, Death by Chocolate, Butter Chicken Biryani.

---

## 7. URU Brewpark (JP Nagar)

**Vibe:** Sprawling 4-Acre Park, Microbrewery & Pet-Friendly Garden  
**Best For:** Outdoor Sunday brunches, pet lovers, and relaxed outdoor squad meetups.

Set across a lush 4-acre property in JP Nagar, **URU Brewpark** features four distinct zones: a Brewery, a Cafe, a Bistro, and a dedicated Park area. It's one of Bangalore's best open-air spots for large weekend gatherings and pet-friendly meetups.
